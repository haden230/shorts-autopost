Shorts Autopost

A personal tool for publishing short educational videos to their creator's own social accounts.

What this is

Shorts Autopost is a small, private command-line tool. It takes short educational videos that its operator has produced themselves and uploads them to that same person's own YouTube and TikTok accounts, using each platform's official API.

It is not a product, a service, or an app that other people sign up for. It runs on one person's computer and posts to one person's accounts.

What it does

  • Reads finished video files from a folder on the operator's own computer.
  • Uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.
  • Attaches a title and description that the operator wrote beforehand.
  • Retrieves view and retention statistics for the operator's own videos, so they can see which ones performed well.

What it does not do

  • It does not access anyone else's account or content.
  • It does not collect, store, or sell anyone's personal information.
  • It does not display advertising and has no analytics or tracking of its own.
  • It does not post automatically without the operator's involvement — every batch is started by hand.
The content itself

The videos are short explanations of everyday science questions — why ice floats, why onions make you cry, why a kettle goes quiet before it boils. All artwork, animation, and background music are generated procedurally by the operator's own code, so no third-party copyrighted material is used.
